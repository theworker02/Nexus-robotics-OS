# Nexus platform values

## Interoperability

One capability layer across heterogeneous robots and robotics stacks.

## Safety

All Nexus-originated physical commands pass through policy and safety enforcement.

## Portability

Skills declare what they need rather than assuming a particular robot.

## Reproducibility

Simulation, containers, replay, manifests, versioned packages, and CI make behavior repeatable.

## Observability

Commands, skills, tasks, state changes, and safety decisions are inspectable.

## Progressive validation

Nexus distinguishes unit-tested, simulation-tested, HIL-tested, hardware-validated, and vendor-validated behavior. These labels are never interchangeable.
