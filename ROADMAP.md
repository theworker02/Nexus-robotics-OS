# Roadmap

## Current — v4.2

Public release maturity: truthful versioning, documentation, compatibility metadata, CI/release gates, GitHub Pages, Rust distribution, and transparent validation reports. This release candidate remains simulation-first and does not claim universal robot, HIL, or physical validation.

## Near term

### v4.3

- Integration hardening and conformance reports.
- Expanded deterministic and adversarial benchmark coverage.
- Community hardware compatibility reports.

### v4.5

- Hardware-in-the-loop expansion where equipment and evidence are available.
- Package registry and signing maturity.
- Additional robotics-stack adapters.

## Long term

### v5.0

- Broader physical hardware validation.
- More advanced multi-robot coordination.
- Vendor-certified integrations where independently supported.
- Production fleet tooling with explicit operational evidence.

## Non-goals

Nexus is not attempting to replace Linux with a custom kernel, provide unrestricted AI motor control, claim universal robot compatibility, claim physical validation without hardware, replace every vendor tool, or treat simulation as proof of mechanical safety.
